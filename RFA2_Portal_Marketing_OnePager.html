RFA-2 Portal

AI-Powered Workers' Compensation Filing for Insurance Carriers, TPAs & Defense Firms
As of March 20, 2026, the NYS WCB no longer accepts paper RFA-2 forms. Every filing must be electronic. The WCB's eCase portal is manual, single-user, and has no AI. RFA-2 Portal transforms your filing workflow from hours to minutes.

The Problem

Your adjusters spend 30-45 minutes per RFA-2 filing — manually reading IME reports, selecting reason codes, typing narratives, cross-referencing WCB rules, and entering data field-by-field in eCase. At 200+ filings per month, that's 100+ hours of adjuster time wasted on data entry. Missed deadlines mean lost claim control.
